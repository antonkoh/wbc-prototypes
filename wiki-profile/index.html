Prototype - hosting policy review submissions

Submitting an instance for review

Two variants of the same user need, for the capacity decision on the submissions story. Both show the manager's side of the hosting policy review.
Full scope
/full
The wiki profile lives in the platform. Two panes: the questionnaire on the Profile tab, and the Review tab with the full submission model.
  • Submission blocked until the wiki profile is saved
  • Manager can cancel an open submission
  • History of past submissions with their states and rejection reasons
MVP scope
/mvp
The questionnaire stays in an external tool. Only the Review tab is built, and a submission either exists or it does not.
  • No wiki profile in the platform, so no dependency on it
  • Manager confirms they filled out the external questionnaire, then submits
  • One submission, no cancelling, no history
