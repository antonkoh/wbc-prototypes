 About this Wikibase Describe your use case here. This is what the review committee reads when you submit this instance to keep it beyond its three months.
Is the data in this Wikibase intended for reuse by other people?*
Wikibase serves different use cases: from sharing data that anyone can rely on to get insights and build useful applications, to data staging and experimentation in an isolated sandbox. As people explore the Wikibase ecosystem, we want to make it easier for them to identify and discover datasets that are actually meant for reuse by audiences beyond their contributing community, taken care of and unlikely to disappear unexpectedly.
Choose the option that best describes the data in this Wikibase.
What is the intended purpose of this Wikibase?*
Please describe how you intend to use this Wikibase in more detail (separate questions about the dataset, intended audience and contributors will follow).
Here are examples of the information we are looking for:
  • I would like to publish a dataset, so I could build an application on top of it.
  • My community would like to collaboratively maintain a dataset on a topic of our interest.
  • I am teaching how to use Wikibase to a group of students and plan to use this instance to showcase its functionality.
  • I am refining a dataset before importing it into Wikidata. It can be deleted once I'm done.
  • I am building a tool for data import into Wikibase and using this Wikibase as an experimentation ground.
