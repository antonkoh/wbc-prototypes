All Wikibases on our platform are temporary by default.

Once you're confident you want to keep this Wikibase longer than 3 months, submit it for review.

Note that some uses can't be approved for a longer stay at all — for example, primarily commercial or promotional use, or content under a license that isn't free and open. The full conditions are in the Hosting Policy. Make sure your use case would be approved before you invest time in building it.

Previously accepted terms of use and hosting policy still apply.
